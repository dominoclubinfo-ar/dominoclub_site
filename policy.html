Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Welcome to Domino Club (“we,” “our,” or “us”). Domino Club is an online multiplayer domino game where you can enjoy Double‑6 and Double‑9 variants, compete with other players, earn coins, and purchase coins via Google Play or watch optional ads. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our application and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using Domino Club, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this privacy policy, you must not use the app.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless and secure multiplayer experience, we collect the following types of information:

a) Google Account Information (Authentication)

Domino Club requires you to sign in with your Google account. When you authenticate, we receive basic profile information from Google, including your:

  • Name (as shown on your Google account)
  • Email address
  • Profile picture URL (if you choose to provide one)

We use this information to create your in‑game identity, display your profile to other players, and prevent fraudulent accounts. We never store your Google password — authentication is handled securely by Google’s OAuth system.

b) Game Data & Usage Information

To enable online multiplayer matches and leaderboards, we automatically collect:

  • Your in‑game progress, match history, and rankings
  • Virtual coin balance and transaction records (purchases, rewards, match wins/losses)
  • Device identifiers (such as Android ID, advertising ID) to maintain session integrity and prevent cheating
  • Game preferences (favourite domino variant, sound settings, etc.)

c) Purchases & Transactions (Google Play Billing)

All coin purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your credit card or payment details. Google Play shares purchase confirmation and transaction IDs with us so we can credit your account with the correct amount of coins. For subscription or one‑time purchase issues, please refer to Google Play’s privacy policy.

d) Advertising Data (Optional Rewarded Ads)

You may watch rewarded video ads to earn free coins. These ads are served by Google AdMob (or similar third‑party ad networks). The ad networks may collect:

  • Device advertising ID (resettable by user)
  • IP address, device model, OS version
  • Approximate location (only if you grant permission; location is never required for gameplay)

We do not directly share your personal information with advertisers, but the ad SDK operates under its own privacy policy. You can opt out of interest‑based ads via your Android device settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • Authentication & account management – to sign you in and keep your profile secure.
  • Multiplayer functionality – to match you with other players, track game results, and maintain fair play.
  • Virtual economy & transactions – to manage coin balances, process purchases, and award ad rewards.
  • App improvement & analytics – to understand how users interact with Domino Club, fix bugs, and improve performance.
  • Compliance & security – to detect fraudulent activity, enforce our terms, and prevent abuse (e.g., bots or coin farming).

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With other players: Your chosen display name and profile picture are visible to other players during matches. Your email address is never shown to them.
  • Service providers: We use Google Play Games Services, Firebase (for real‑time database and analytics), and Google AdMob. These providers may access your data only to perform tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for other purposes.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, subpoena, or governmental request, we may disclose your information to comply with legal obligations.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before any such transfer and the privacy policy remains applicable.

4. Children’s Privacy

Domino Club is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us, and we will take steps to delete such information. All users must be at least 13 years old to use the app, and by signing in with Google you confirm you meet this age requirement.

5. Security of Your Data

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

🔐 Your responsibilities: Keep your Google account credentials secure. Because Domino Club relies on Google authentication, the security of your account also depends on using a strong password and two‑factor authentication where available. If you suspect any unauthorised access, change your Google password immediately and contact us.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR in Europe, CCPA in California, LGPD in Brazil), you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & portability: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt‑out of ads personalisation: On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.
  • Withdraw consent: You may stop using the app and delete your account at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 8. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with Domino Club services. If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain certain information for legal, fraud prevention, or legitimate operational reasons (e.g., match history for leaderboard integrity may be kept anonymised).
